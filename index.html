Eye Tracking Control

This feature uses your webcam to track where you are looking and steer the snake with your gaze. Your camera feed is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded or stored.

  • Camera access required (browser will ask)
  • All processing happens on your device
  • No video is recorded or transmitted
  • Disable at any time via the Eye Track button
